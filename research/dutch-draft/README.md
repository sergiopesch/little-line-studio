## Offline checks and text inventories

- `node scripts/verify-dutch-draft.mjs --complete` checks the locale, draft status, source and geometry fingerprints, all lesson fields, ordered stages and phases, and every narration and hint string. `--complete` confirms text coverage only; it is **not** a native review, audio approval or release gate.
- `node scripts/export-dutch-draft.mjs --write-catalogue` writes the unactivated `src/dutch-lessons.nl-NL.json` copy only after the complete source passes those checks. `src/dutch-lessons.js` can apply the text without replacing geometry or lesson mechanics, but is not wired into the picker. Tests compare the runtime copy to the verified source and reject missing text.
- `node scripts/plan-dutch-pilot.mjs` prints a text-only review document: the greeting plus 14 varied Cat clips, selected preview voice, exact texts and digest. The complete Cat lesson has 42 instruction/hint keys plus the greeting. The sample cannot establish full-lesson audio quality.
- `node scripts/plan-dutch-catalogue.mjs` prints a text-only inventory of the greeting and all 1,460 keys, their source texts, selected preview identity and deterministic digest. It makes no provider calls, uses no credentials and contains no provider requests. Counts are Unicode code points, not a credit quote or approved budget. Any text change changes the digest.
- `node --test tests/dutch-preparation.test.mjs` verifies the complete source/export relationship, unchanged geometry, missing-field rejection, selected safety and optionality cases, UI coverage, and the pending permission state. `npm run check` is the repository-wide validation. No automated check establishes natural Dutch pronunciation or educational quality.

Native Dutch review should compare every lesson's wording with the actual demonstration, including directional instructions, hidden edges, safety and optional details, and review the interface and accessibility labels in context. Check the Cat pilot for natural Netherlands usage and, after authorized recordings exist, pronunciation of *snorhaar*, *boogje*, colour names and the spoken letter W. Record reviewer, date, corrections and precise scope; assistant review is not independent native-speaker review.

Short clips and perceptual differences need human judgment; matching a filter target alone is not acceptance.
3. Have a human listen to every pilot clip for Dutch pronunciation, cadence, intelligibility, clipping, silence and consistent perceived loudness. Record device, headphones or speakers, reviewer and findings. Review corrections before any further generation; do not claim a full-catalogue audition from the pilot.

Confirm music continues from the same position while voice starts and ducks smoothly, recovers afterwards and loops without a perceptible gap. Capture measured timing/volume or media-state evidence alongside a human listening log; automated tests do not substitute for listening.
