# Netherlands Dutch — nl-NL

Working editorial guide, grounded in the current Cat script. Native-speaker review and human listening remain pending. Follow the [shared workflow](README.md).

## Audience and voice

Use je/jij for the child in the current style and jullie for the family together where appropriate. Adult guidance addresses the supervising adult; name the drawing explicitly where “je kind” could change the referent.

Use the approved Netherlands Dutch voice. Listen for natural compound-word stress, “snorhaar” and “driehoekje”, and a gentle rather than hurried delivery of repeated marks.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Keep “tekenen” and “kleuren” distinct. Use “lijn”, “streepje” and “boogje” for the relevant gesture, with diminutives where useful for scale. “Meer tijd” remains an unlimited hold.

“Je mag hem ook weglaten” preserves permission to skip. “Witte plekjes mogen ook bij de vacht horen” welcomes uncoloured paper without demanding a matching finish.
