# Spain Spanish — es-ES

Working editorial guide, grounded in the current Cat script. Native-speaker review and human listening remain pending. Follow the [shared workflow](README.md).

## Audience and voice

Use tú for the child and vosotros forms when addressing the family together, as in “Elegid” and “Mirad”. A singular adult note can address one supervising adult; the audience determines the form.

Use the approved Spain voice and local letter-name pronunciation. Check the natural flow of “uve doble”, “trazos” and longer colour explanations; do not compress delivery to match English duration.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Keep the established choices “añade”, “rosa”, “hacia fuera”, “no hay prisa” and “uve doble”. Treat these as project preferences, not a claim that alternatives are always incorrect.

“Añade el otro ojito al otro lado de la cara” explains placement independently of the English sentence structure. Preserve the space between the eyes.
