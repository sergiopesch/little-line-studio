# Italy Italian — it-IT

Working editorial guide, grounded in the current Cat script. Native-speaker review and human listening remain pending. Follow the [shared workflow](README.md).

## Audience and voice

Use tu for the child; current family invitations use plural forms such as “Guardate”. Adult guidance may address one adult directly. Preserve agreement when changing the noun or subject.

Listen for stress in longer words, natural phrasing of “doppia vu”, and a clear pause between movement and reassurance. Let the recording determine spoken duration.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Keep “tratto” or “segno” according to the movement, “colorare” for colouring and “baffo” for the pictured whisker. The local mouth comparison is “doppia vu”. Avoid mechanically replacing every English “mark” with one word.

“Puoi anche non disegnarla” keeps an optional line genuinely optional. “Musetto” in the invitation can be warmer than repeating the same literal noun in every field.
