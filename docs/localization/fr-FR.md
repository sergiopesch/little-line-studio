# France French — fr-FR

Working editorial guide, grounded in the current Cat script. Native-speaker review and human listening remain pending. Follow the [shared workflow](README.md).

## Audience and voice

Address the child with tu. The parent notes use vous; keep that adult guidance separate from spoken child instructions. Preserve accents, elisions and readable punctuation.

Listen for natural liaison, the letter W comparison and pauses in longer colouring instructions. Avoid forcing French clauses into English rhythm.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Use the established “trait” for an individual drawing mark, “colorier” for colouring and “roux” for the example cat’s fur. “Plus de temps” means an unlimited hold, not a fixed extra duration.

“Une seule courbe convient aussi” preserves permission to simplify the mouth. A sentence can be reordered while keeping the same drawing relationship.
