# Human and physical-device acceptance

Use the generated worksheet for the exact lesson and locale under review. Record the app revision from `/release.json`, the packet fingerprints, reviewer/date and device/OS/browser. These checks supplement the automated suite; an empty worksheet is not a pass.

## Language and teaching

- Read captions, spoken text, hints and adult notes together while watching the corresponding stage. Check regional wording, forms of address, spatial directions and natural pronunciation.
- Follow the sequence with every optional detail omitted. Later instructions must still make sense. Confirm the invitation to choose colours, pause, take more time and explore.
- Flag specific text or recordings by stage/message ID and hash. Describe the problem and proposed wording. A spoken edit needs a matching recording before publication.

## Audio on physical devices

Cover an iPhone/iPad with Safari, Android with Chrome, and a desktop browser. Record whether the output uses built-in speakers or headphones and the device/app volume settings. Start quietly and choose a comfortable level; do not infer perceived loudness from the HTML volume value.

- Start through the adult gate. Listen for clear narration, comfortable pace, intact word beginnings/endings and sufficient quiet practice.
- Follow instruction → quiet practice → Help → Hear the hint → Stop hint → return. Music should remain continuous and recede beneath speech without an abrupt jump or restart.
- Listen across a full music loop and several instruction boundaries. Flag pumping, clicks, sudden changes, masking or harshness.
- Test Pause, mute, settings, finish, app switching, device lock/unlock and interruption by another audio source. Confirm immediate silence where required, no catch-up and explicit supervised resumption.
- Test unavailable narration and a weak connection. Written instructions and supervised recovery should remain understandable.

## Accessibility and family use

- Use the keyboard through entry, catalogue, lesson, Help and settings. Check visible focus, dialog focus restoration and access to every control.
- Check enlarged text/zoom and portrait/landscape layouts. Use reduced motion and still demonstrations.
- Use VoiceOver or another screen reader on the target device. Check language switching, names, reading order and status announcements; record the assistive technology and version.
- With a supervising adult, observe whether a child understands each small step and can use More time, Pause, Replay, optional details and open exploration comfortably. Record observations without child names, photos, recordings or other identifying information.

Separate a completed review from approval to publish a content change. Retain unresolved issues and retest their exact revised text/audio; do not replace a failed result with a new unannotated packet.
