# Germany German — de-DE

Working editorial guide, grounded in the current Cat script. Native-speaker review and human listening remain pending. Follow the [shared workflow](README.md).

## Audience and voice

Use du for the child. Current adult notes use Sie, while family-facing UI sometimes uses ihr. Keep the intended audience explicit instead of globally replacing one form with another.

Use the approved replacement German voice. Check consonant clarity, compound words such as “Schnurrhaar”, and quiet short hints. The earlier quality concern makes real listening especially important; louder alone does not mean better.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Prefer concrete “Linie”, “Strich”, “Bogen”, “Dreieck” and “Schnurrhaar” in their drawing contexts. “Ausmalen” and “Zeichnen” have distinct uses. Preserve “Mehr Zeit” as the control’s exact reference in hints.

“Deine beiden Seiten dürfen unterschiedlich sein” keeps the invitation to differ. Break up a long sentence when useful, while preserving its action and direction.
