# Scottish English voice — en-GB-x-scotland

Working editorial guide, grounded in the current Cat script. Native-speaker review and human listening remain pending. Follow the [shared workflow](README.md).

## Audience and voice

Use the shared English wording for children and adults. This application identifier represents a Scottish English voice, not Scots or Scottish Gaelic.

Preserve the approved Scottish voice. Check sustained warmth, short-hint endings and natural pauses with a Scottish English listener; do not exaggerate the accent.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Keep concrete, broadly understandable English. Avoid adding “wee” or phonetic spellings simply to signal a region. A regional expression should earn its place through clarity and listener review.

“One curve is fine too” should sound like permission rather than correction. Preserve the same short instruction and fuller optional hint.
