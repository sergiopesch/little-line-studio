# British English — en-GB

Working editorial guide, grounded in the current Cat script. Native-speaker review and human listening remain pending. Follow the [shared workflow](README.md).

## Audience and voice

Speak directly to the child with “you”; use “we” for a demonstrated invitation. Adult notes address the supervising adult separately.

Keep the approved British voice and a warm, unhurried delivery. Listen for clipped short hints, an overly emphatic W comparison, and abrupt transitions into quiet practice.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Keep the existing project spelling “colour”, “colouring” and “cosy”. Use plain shape and movement words before technical terms. “More time” is an unlimited hold.

“A tiny oval is enough” provides reassurance without demanding precision. “Your two sides can be different” preserves choice.
