## Audience and voice

Use the current shared English child/adult distinction. This is an Irish English voice option; it is not an Irish-language translation.

Preserve the approved Irish voice. Evaluate its natural cadence and the pronunciation of “whiskers”, “oval” and the letter W with an Irish English listener. Do not copy the British recording’s timing mechanically.

## Vocabulary and adaptation

Retain the shared English spelling and accessible drawing vocabulary. Add regional expressions only after checking that they help the intended families; avoid invented dialect spelling.

Keep “A little gap is fine” and “You can leave it out” natural in delivery, with the same optionality as the source.
