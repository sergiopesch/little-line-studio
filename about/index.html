Hi, I’m Sergio.

A dad, a husband, and someone who loves turning ideas into useful things.

My name is Sergio Peschiera. In my day job, I help nonprofit organisations make the most of technology. In my own time, I build and share projects that explore what these tools can make possible.

Why I’m building Trazolino

I want to give children and their grown-ups a simple way to enjoy making something together. A little guidance on screen, a pencil and paper, and plenty of time to try things their own way.

Trazolino is my exploration of how AI and thoughtful design can help make that experience available in more languages. The lessons are prepared in advance, a grown-up stays alongside, and the drawing happens on paper.

My hope is simple: more creativity, more confidence to have a go, and more little moments together.
