Hi, I’m Sergio.

I’m a dad who discovered that keeping one little person entertained during half-term is a full-time job. 😅

Why I’m building Trazolino

I started looking for simple, creative things my daughter and I could do together at home. That’s how Trazolino came about.

It’s a free, work-in-progress drawing teacher for children and their grown-ups. The site shows each step, but the drawing happens on paper.

One of my favourite parts is the Venezuelan Spanish voice. A small way to help my little one stay connected to her roots. 🇻🇪

I’d love to add more languages, handwriting and other pen-and-paper activities for families around the world.
