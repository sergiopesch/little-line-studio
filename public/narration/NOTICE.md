The Spanish, French, Irish and Scottish catalogues are released with independent native-speaker review and complete human listening still pending. French text has received assistant editorial review. Technical checks, including complete decoding, do not establish accent, pronunciation, educational suitability or perceptual quality.

English narration was prepared on 26 October 2026; the completed Spanish and additional language catalogues were prepared on 28 October using the owner’s paid account. This notice records provenance; it does not grant rights beyond the applicable [ElevenLabs terms](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use).

The assets are fixed MP3 files served from this website. Provider output was loudness-normalised to a -20 LUFS target with a -2 dB true-peak ceiling, encoded at 44,100 Hz mono/128 kbit/s, and stripped of incidental metadata. Playback uses 0.9× speed with pitch preservation requested from the browser. Device volume and browser support still affect the listening experience; these settings are not calibrated sound-pressure limits.

The source manifest retains exact authored transcripts, content hashes, measured native durations, provider/model/voice identities and generation/encoding settings. Identical generation requests share an asset. Audio is loaded on demand; no API key or generation service runs in the browser. No text, microphone audio or information about a child is sent to ElevenLabs during a lesson. The website cannot see, assess or judge the paper drawing.

The complete English curriculum has thirty authored story-and-crayon packs: ten Basic, ten Intermediate and ten Expert. Every drawing has its own fixed narration, hints, story and original instrumental composition. A consistent teacher voice within each locale keeps the experience familiar across levels; it is not a live model or a clone of a child or family member.

Optional background music has separate [music provenance](/music/NOTICE.md). Local processing keeps each source hash, crop, normalisation mode and loop recipe in its provenance; reviewed per-source processing choices do not change the voice or playback controls. Music is enabled by default, but lesson audio begins only from the supervising adult's Begin gesture; explicit sound checks require a separate adult gesture. The adult can turn music off before beginning or mute it during the lesson. Enabled music continues across ordinary lesson steps, lowers to 32% of the selected gain beneath teaching with a 200 ms attack, and recovers over 1,200 ms afterwards. Pause, mute, dialogs, help, hidden tabs and safety interruptions still silence it immediately. Short recorded-voice edge fades soften starts and ends. Physical listening to every clip and browser/codec loop-seam quality remain unqualified. The voice, playback speed and soundtrack have not been shown to improve drawing outcomes in a trial of this application.
