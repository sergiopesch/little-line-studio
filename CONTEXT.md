# Trazolino

Parent-supervised drawing practice on paper, guided by an authored demonstration and prepared narration.

## Language

**Authored lesson**: A reviewed sequence of observation, drawing, colouring and exploration steps, with localized text and prepared media.
_Avoid_: Generated session, chat response

**Family session**: A temporary browser experience in which an adult and child follow an authored lesson together. It does not create a child profile.
_Avoid_: Child account, learner record

**Supervision acknowledgement**: The adult's local declaration that they will stay for the session. It does not establish identity, verified consent or continuous presence.
_Avoid_: Authentication, parental verification

**Quiet practice**: Space to draw after instruction and demonstration, with unlimited family holds and no judgement of the paper drawing.
_Avoid_: Deadline, assessment

**Open exploration**: The final stage in which the family can keep drawing indefinitely and choose when to finish.
_Avoid_: Automatic completion

**Rehearsal**: An isolated simulation of authored lesson rules using public content and simulated completion events. It never represents a real family session or proof that media played.
_Avoid_: Listening review, parent approval

**Workspace grant**: A host-selected set of public lessons, locales and operations available through the local agent interface. A request cannot enlarge it.
_Avoid_: Verified parental consent
